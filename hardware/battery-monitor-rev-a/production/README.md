# Battery monitor rev-a: production package

Board: ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 + Ra-02 battery voltage monitor, 4 layers, 62 x 50 mm, 1.6 mm, HASL (JLCPCB standard, no surcharge), 66 footprints (62 BOM references, of them 59 soldered parts in the position file + J101 wire pads + TP102/TP103 test pads, + 4 mounting holes H1–H4), all on the top side, hand assembly. Built 2026-10-15, reworked 2026-10-18, balanced 2026-10-20, revised in review round r6 (2026-10-22), silkscreen reworked in r7, top silk texts edited by the owner in r8, parts re-sourced to Ukrainian stock in the IMRAD-stock round (2026-10-25: F101 and R511 footprints changed, MPN fields), U201 thermal-pad holes 0.20 -> 0.30 mm in the JLCPCB-standard round (2026-10-26), re-exported 2026-10-26 with KiCad 10.0.6 `kicad-cli` from the installed board `battery-monitor-rev-a.kicad_pcb` SHA256 `cf1b4365...` (full hash in `../evidence/jlc-standard-20261026/report.md`; every file of this package except the purchase workbook: fabrication files, assembly drawings, renders, STEP, BOM, position file); DRC with schematic parity 0 errors / 0 unconnected / 0 parity, 7 accepted `lib_footprint_mismatch` warnings (J201, U201, U401, L101, TP102, TP103, R511: board silk edited against the library footprints; never run "Update footprints from library" on them).

## What to upload

`01-gerbers-for-order/battery-monitor-rev-a-gerbers.zip` to JLCPCB as a standard 4-layer board with the quote-page defaults (bare PCB + top stencil, no surcharge). The quote-page table, the expected charges, the design facts, drill counts and the answers to engineering questions are in `01-gerbers-for-order/README-FABRICATION.md`.

**No assembly service for Ukraine:** JLCPCB offers no PCB assembly for delivery to Ukraine and LCSC takes no orders from Ukraine. Order the bare PCB and the stencil only; the parts are bought in Ukraine / AliExpress and soldered by hand.

A library fix (project-local copies of these seven footprints with the edited silk) is a rev-b item.

**Inline battery-lead fuse (owner wiring, required):** a 2 A MINI/ATO blade fuse (32 V DC rated) in an inline holder in the battery + lead within 0.2 m of the + clip. F101 on the board (Littelfuse NANO2 0451002.MRL since 2026-10-25) interrupts up to 50 A at 125 V AC/DC and 300 A at 32 V DC (Littelfuse 451/453 datasheet, interrupting ratings) and stays secondary protection; a 12/24 V battery can deliver more into a shorted input part or a shorted lead. 48 V part swap: fuse rated >= 80 V DC. Text in `03-documentation/ASSEMBLY-NOTES.md` section 3; purchase lines П8/П9 on the workbook sheet `Поза платою`.

**Bottom silk date:** the bottom silkscreen reads `battery-monitor rev A` and `2026-10-23  4L` (date of the r7 silk rework; the top carries `rev A`). Later changes (the same day the F.Fab-only board `22731ea4` and the owner's top-silk edit r8, board `f26090a2`; on 2026-10-25 the IMRAD-stock round, board `98f3c7e2`) are identified by this package and the board hash, not by the bottom silk.
